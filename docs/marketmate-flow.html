MarketMate — how it works

Film your stall and say your prices. Get a checked price list, a printed price board, a shop page and a POS file. Customers see today's checked prices.

SellerCustomer Meta AI modelModal (our server) Plain code (no AI)Check / flag

1. The big picture

Seller's phone

  • Films stall + speaks prices
  • Fixes flagged values, approves
  • Prints board, publishes, exports

Customer's phone

  • Scans QR on price board
  • Searches, compares, plans budget
  • Calls / chats the seller
⇄

MarketMate web app Modal

  • Website pages (seller + customer)
  • ffmpeg: video → audio + frames
  • Checks that keep numbers honest
  • Database: shops, price lists, history
  • PDF, shop page, CSV, search, budget maths
  • Daily cost cap

Free *.modal.run address — no domain to buy. Nothing is trained; we only call models.

⇄

Meta AI API

  • Voice Transcribe — speech → text (Tamil + English mixed)
  • Muse Spark — reads the text into items/prices, looks at frames, understands customer questions
  • SAM 3.1 — cuts each product out of the real video frame

Key is in .env — verified working 27 Oct.

6. What we promise — and what we don't

We promise
Every price comes from the seller's own words, with proof. Nothing reaches customers until the seller approves it.
We don't do
Guess weight from video · grade freshness for customers · AI-drawn photos or prices · payments or delivery.
Privacy
Audio goes to Meta's API (cloud). Raw video is not published. Shop location is an area name, not GPS.
