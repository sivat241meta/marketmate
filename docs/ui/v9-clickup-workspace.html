MarketMate · design v9 · ClickUp colours and layout (preview)

A clean workspace for the seller.
A fresh product grid for the customer.

The seller side now uses ClickUp's colours and layout: ClickUp purple, a dark top bar with a centred search, a dark icon sidebar, list views grouped by status with coloured status pills, List/Board view tabs with a purple underline, and "+ New" rows at the bottom of each group. The customer side borrows quick-commerce product tiles like Zepto's: square white photo tiles, the product cut out on white, an ADD button on the photo's corner, and a green price label. The same ClickUp purple ties the two together. Every small text colour passes the 4.5:1 readability check. Fonts stay as they are (Plus Jakarta Sans + Noto Tamil), so nothing new is downloaded.

Photos like quick-commerce, but real

Today a photo is a crop of the video frame, so neighbouring items, boxes and even people get in. v8 cuts the product out with the SAM 3.1 mask (Meta's own parser package) and places it on white with a soft shadow. It's the seller's real produce, not a stock or AI-made image.

  • No product found → no photo, so a frame with a face is never used. In your live test, the "tomato" photo was the front-camera selfie; SAM found no tomato in it.
  • Faces are never published: a frame with a person in it is skipped.
  • The seller can still pick 1 of 3, or take a new photo.
