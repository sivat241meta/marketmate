MarketMate · v4, quick-commerce study (now with Meta Blue)

The energy of quick-commerce apps (gradient headers, photo tiles, ADD buttons sitting on the photo, a floating cart, rails), applied to a real stall. 3 original palettes named after vegetables. All product photos and most prices come from your 2 real shop videos and the kitchen video. Orders and earnings are sample data.

Feels like apps they useGradient header, a 4-across category grid, an ADD button on the photo, a floating cart: the same muscle memory as instant-delivery apps. Every colour and shape here is our own.
Camera with price stickersLive mode pins each heard price onto the produce in the camera view, like a sticker. Prices it isn't sure about get a yellow "?" sticker to check after.
Swipe to checkUnclear prices come as a card deck: one big question with two huge answers. Faster than a form, and works with one thumb at the stall.
Your day, as a storyDay close is a story-style recap ("₹6,420 today, beans sold most") that the owner can share on WhatsApp. Excel is one tap away.
