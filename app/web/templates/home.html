Lost your link?

Your seller link is the only way back into your shop. There are no passwords and no SMS codes, so MarketMate cannot send you the link.

You can only find it again in the WhatsApp message you sent to yourself from your dashboard or Settings ("Send my link to my WhatsApp"), or on the printed "Keep this safe" card. Keep one of them safe.
